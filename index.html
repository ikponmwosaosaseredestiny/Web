About Me

Passionate About Sustainable Engineering and Innovative Research

Osasere Destiny Ikponmwosa is a self-motivated, determined and enthusiastic Structural Engineering graduate with a passionate interest in pursuing a career in research in the field of Sustainable Materials and Structures, Advanced Solid Mechanics, Structural Analysis & Design, Sustainable Construction and Renewable Energy.
